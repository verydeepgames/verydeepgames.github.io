# Privacy Policy — Caravane 1352 Last updated: October 17, 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how Caravane 1352 handles your information. ## Personal Information You do not need to create an account or provide any personal information to use the application. ## Data Stored on Your Device Your saved games, settings, and player notes are stored locally on your device and are not transferred to us or third parties. This data is deleted when you uninstall the application, subject to your device's backup and restoration settings. ## Advertising The application uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including optional rewarded ads. Google and its advertising partners may process device information, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, and ad interactions to provide and measure advertisements. Where required, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages advertising consent and provides privacy choices. ## Usage Statistics The application uses Firebase Analytics to collect gameplay statistics, including journeys, trading, encounters, and other game events. These statistics are analyzed in aggregate to understand how the game is played and improve its gameplay and balance. ## Crash Reports The application uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect technical information about errors and crashes. These reports help us identify problems and improve the application's stability. ## Data Sharing and Security Google processes data through AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics to provide these services. We do not share data with other third parties beyond Google and its advertising partners as described above. Data transmitted to Google services is protected using encrypted connections. For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy). ## Data Retention Data stored locally remains on your device until it is deleted or the application is uninstalled. Data processed by Google services is retained according to their applicable retention policies and settings. ## Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published in the updated policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. ## Contact If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: northmeregames@gmail.com
