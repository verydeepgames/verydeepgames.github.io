Privacy Policy — Caravane 1352

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Caravane 1352 is a mobile game published by Very Deep Games. This policy explains which data may be processed when you use the game and for what purposes.

Data processed

Caravane 1352 does not require a user account and does not directly collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or other personal content.

The game uses Google services for advertising, audience measurement, and technical diagnostics. These services may process, among other things, advertising or technical identifiers, device and app information, IP address, approximate location information derived from the network connection, and data relating to your use of the application.

Advertising

Caravane 1352 uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded ads that the player chooses to watch.

In regions where required, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents consent choices relating to advertising and data processing.

These choices can be reviewed or changed from Settings → Privacy when this option is available.

Audience measurement and diagnostics

Caravane 1352 uses Firebase Analytics to measure aggregate game usage and Firebase Crashlytics to detect and diagnose errors and crashes.

This information is used to understand how the game operates, identify technical issues, and improve the player experience.

Data sharing

Google receives the data necessary to operate AdMob, Google UMP, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics. Google and its advertising partners may also process certain data in accordance with the applicable consent choices.

Caravane 1352 does not sell game data to third parties.

Location

Caravane 1352 does not request permission to access the device's precise geographic location.

Data retention

Data processed by Google services is retained in accordance with the policies and retention periods defined by Google for the relevant services. Caravane 1352 does not maintain a user account database.

Your choices

Where required by law, you can accept, refuse, or change choices relating to advertising data processing through Google's consent form.

Refusing advertising consent does not prevent you from using Caravane 1352.

Google services

For more information about how Google processes data in its advertising and Firebase services, please refer to Google's privacy policies and user information pages.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game's features or the services it uses change. The last-updated date shown at the top of this page identifies the applicable version.
